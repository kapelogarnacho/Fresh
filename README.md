# Fresh Entertainment

> A complete social entertainment platform with a 3D interactive globe, mini‑games, live market, and community features.

Fresh Entertainment is a futuristic web application that combines social networking, gaming, media sharing, and real‑time interaction. Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Three.js, it offers a neon‑themed hub where users can register, build profiles, connect with friends, play games, share content, and explore an immersive 3D globe.

---

## ✨ Features

### 👤 User & Profile Management
- **Registration & Login** – simple sign‑up with username, email, and country.
- **Profile Modal** – view and edit your username, points, followers, following, and likes.
- **User Agreement** – two‑step agreement: accept sharing policies and zero‑tolerance warning (optional interests).
- **Explore Page** – discover other users, follow them, view profiles, chat, and call.

### 🌍 Interactive 3D Globe
- Realistic Earth with clouds, particle rings, and a glitch‑effect gate.
- **Transmission Button** – sends a visual pulse across the globe and awards points.
- Fully rotatable and zoomable with OrbitControls.

### 🎮 Arcade Mini‑Games
- **Neon Clicker** – tap to increase your score.
- **Tic‑Tac‑Toe** – classic game with score tracking.
- **Neon Jump** – jump over obstacles with spacebar.
- **Baby Commando** – twin‑stick shooter with keyboard controls.
- **Memory Match** – flip cards to find matching pairs.

### 📸 Media & Content
- **Photo Gallery** – upload images with captions; auto‑scrolling gallery.
- **Video Feed** – upload and play videos; TikTok‑like scroll.
- **Reviews** – post and view community reviews.

### 📈 Live Market Simulation
- Real‑time stock ticker with random price changes.
- Awards activity points for market watching.

### 🎉 Party Mode
- Toggle neon visual effects, floating emojis, and a mouse trail.
- Counts party activations and awards bonus points.

### 🔒 Secure Vault
- Enter a username and password (demo: `admin` / `fresh2026`) to unlock.
- Visual scanner line and glitch effects.

### 💬 Communication
- Direct **Chat** button (links to `chatmd.html`).
- **Call** button (links to `free.com` for offline calling after following users).

### 🧩 Additional Tools
- **Scanner Tool** – QR code scan, item search, and screen mirror (simulated).
- **Pro Features** – showcase locked premium content with unlock button.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies

- **Frontend**: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6)
- **3D Rendering**: Three.js (r128) with OrbitControls
- **Icons**: Font Awesome 6
- **Database**: Firebase Firestore (optional)
- **Other**: Service Worker (for PWA support)
